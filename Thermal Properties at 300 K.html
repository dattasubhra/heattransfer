Thermal properties of common materials near 300 K

Conductivity k, heat capacity per unit volume ρcp and thermal diffusivity α = k/(ρcp) for metals, other solids, liquids and gases. Select a column heading to sort.

Values are at 300 K, and gases at 1 atm, unless the row says otherwise. ρcp and α are computed from the listed ρ, cp and k.

For a gas, ρcp is proportional to pressure, so α varies inversely with pressure.

Sources: standard heat transfer property tables (Incropera et al., Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer, Appendix A) for most rows. Rows marked “typical” are representative handbook values for materials whose properties vary with grade or composition.
